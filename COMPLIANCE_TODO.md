# Hackathon Compliance Checklist

**Complete these checks manually before submitting your project.**

## Employment Contract & Employer Connections

- [ ] **Employment contract review** — Confirm your employment contract does not give your employer rights over personal projects built outside work hours (this hackathon project).
- [ ] **Household member check** — Verify no household member works for AWS, Devpost, or any affiliate/sponsor of the hackathon.
- [ ] **Judge conflict check** — Confirm your employer does not employ any judge participating in this hackathon.
- [ ] **Final sign-off** — Contract reviewed and all boxes above confirmed clear before submission.
